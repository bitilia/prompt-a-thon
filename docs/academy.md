# Academy

[Wiki home](README.md) · [prompt-a-thon.bitilia.com](https://prompt-a-thon.bitilia.com)

The academy is a list of modules shown on the public home page and under `/dashboard/academy`. Each item is a video embed, a link, or a short reading. Progress is a per-user completion flag. Completing items does not unlock submission.

Shipped modules are beginner material about what AI is, how to prompt, pitfalls, and ethics. Several items embed YouTube via `youtube-nocookie.com` or link out to IBM, learnprompting.org, and the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. Those sites are not part of this project. Links can break, and the pages can change. Replace them in Admin → Academy before you treat the academy as your course.

The content security policy allows YouTube and, on the admin security map only, the CDN scripts that map uses. Other pages do not get a broad script CDN.
